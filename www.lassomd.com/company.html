Eric Bunnell

Chief Executive Officer

Eric Bunnell

Eric Bunnell is the Founder and CEO of ClinicSoftware.ai, a marketing enablement platform for independent clinics that is disrupting the 20-year standard of outsourcing marketing to agencies. Growing up in a family of healthcare professionals and entrepreneurs, Eric was quick to realize that independent clinics are dangerously dependent on outsourcing operations to clinic-specific vendors.

In 2018, Eric made it his mission to repair the dependence healthcare practices have on outsourced services, starting with the least trustworthy vertical: clinic growth. After spending two years fine tuning the product, Eric officially launched ClinicSoftware.ai in May 2020. Since then, ClinicSoftware.ai has enabled quality, affordable, and transparent marketing to over 500 independent clinics across 44 states.
