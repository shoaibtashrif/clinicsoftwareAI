Content FAQs

How much does a photo & video shoot cost for a independent clinic?

Photo shoots are included in our Elevate plans and up, while video shoots are available starting from our Captivate plans. For those not on a ClinicSoftware.ai plan, al-la-carte pricing ranges from $3,000 to $10,000.

What types of photos & videos does ClinicSoftware.ai create?

We specialize in a variety of content types, including lifestyle shots, headshots, office panoramas, short promotional videos, and patient testimonials. Our goal is to capture the essence of your practice and help you connect with your audience.

How long does it take to complete a video shoot? 

Expect a photo shoot to last around 3 hours and a video shoot about 5 hours. If you're opting for both, it will be a full day. Staff are needed for specific segments, but doctors should plan to be available most of the day.

What prep work is required before my video shoot? 

Preparation is key for a successful shoot. The most important step is to coordinating your patient testimonials in advance. Next is choosing  a non-production day with no scheduled patient appointments, and ensuring your office is clean and presentable. Staff will be needed for specific segments, while doctors should plan to be available for most of the day. We'll provide a detailed timesheet to keep everyone on track.

What is the ClinicSoftware.ai Creator Network? Who are the Creators?

The ClinicSoftware.ai Creator Network is our elite team of highly skilled photographers and videographers. Each Creator is rigorously vetted and trained to produce exceptional content that elevates your independent clinic. They're the reason we can offer top-notch visuals without the hefty price tag or hassle.
