FAQs

Does Online Scheduling integrate directly with my Practice Management Software (PMS)?

Yes. ClinicSoftware.ai Online Scheduling integrates with virtually every independent clinic management system, including Dentrix, Dentrix Ascend, Open Clinic, Eaglesoft, Denticon, Curve, Easy Clinic, PracticeWorks, and 200+ others. Appointments are automatically written back to your PMS calendar in real time - no duplicate entry, no manual reconciliation. Optional confirmation workflows can be enabled if you prefer additional schedule control.

Can patients book directly from Google?

Yes. Patients can book from your Google Business Profile, website, and digital campaigns — creating a seamless experience across all channels.

Will this reduce phone calls?

Yes. Many routine bookings (cleanings, new patient exams) can be handled automatically, reducing front desk interruptions and freeing your team for higher-value tasks.

Can I control which appointments are available online?

Absolutely. You control visit types, providers, time blocks, buffers, and scheduling rules.

What makes ClinicSoftware.ai different from other online scheduling tools?

Unlike standalone scheduling tools, ClinicSoftware.ai Online Scheduling is fully PMS-integrated and connected to your broader growth system — including marketing campaigns, AI receptionist, and lead nurturing workflows.
