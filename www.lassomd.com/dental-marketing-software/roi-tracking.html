Clinic Growth Software FAQs

How does ClinicSoftware.ai help my staff schedule new patient leads?

Our Patient Leads Dashboard provides your front desk staff with a streamlined list of unscheduled patient leads, synced in real-time with your Practice Management System. The list automatically updates, removing leads once they've scheduled an appointment. This allows your staff to focus on converting leads more efficiently, all in under 5 minutes a day. Plus, if you miss a call, our system auto-texts the lead to keep them engaged.

How does ClinicSoftware.ai track the exact ROI from marketing? 

ClinicSoftware.ai's ROI Dashboard syncs directly with your Practice Management System, providing you with the exact adjusted production revenue for each converted lead. We focus solely on new patients generated from the marketing activities we're monitoring, offering you a precise and transparent ROI.

Why is ClinicSoftware.ai's ROI Dashboard more accurate than other clinic growth companies? 

While other companies often rely on Google Analytics and make revenue extrapolations, we take a different approach. Our dashboard shows you the exact revenue each new patient lead generates, without any guesswork or extrapolations. This level of transparency sets us apart and allows you to track ROI from any marketing company, not just ClinicSoftware.ai.

Why is Patient Acquisition Cost important to a independent clinic?

Knowing your Patient Acquisition Cost (PAC) is crucial for optimizing your marketing spend. A lower PAC means a higher ROI, allowing you to allocate your budget more effectively. By tracking PAC, you can identify the point of diminishing returns and make data-driven decisions to maximize profitability.

Which Practice Management Systems does ClinicSoftware.ai integrate with?

ClinicSoftware.ai's ClinicSoftware software integrates with virtually every independent clinic management system, including Dentrix, Dentrix Ascend, Open Clinic, Eaglesoft, Denticon, Curve, Easy Clinic, PracticeWorks, and 200+ others. The ROI Dashboard syncs directly with your PMS to pull actual adjusted production revenue for each new patient - so your ROI numbers reflect real dollars, not estimates. If you don't see your system listed, contact us; chances are we support it.
