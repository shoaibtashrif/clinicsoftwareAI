The Challenge

Stand Out in a Competitive Market

Shore Smiles needed more than just a website—they needed a complete rebrand and a powerful digital presence to attract and convert more of the right new patients. Their goals were clear:

  • Showcase their passion for clinicianry and patient care
  • Highlight their vibrant personalities and office culture
  • Stand out in the competitive Virginia Beach market
  • Partner with a transparent marketing agency after past disappointments
  • Generate at least 60+ new patients per month

That’s when they turned to ClinicSoftware.ai to craft a custom marketing strategy built for growth.

The Solution

A High-Performance Digital Ecosystem

We didn’t just build a website—we created a new brand identity from the bottom up and an efficient growth engine that drives results. Here’s how:

  • Website Overhaul: We launched a stunning, mobile-responsive site with clear navigation and high-impact CTAs, ensuring a seamless experience for both new and returning patients.
  • On-Site Photo & Video Shoot: Our professional shoot captured the warmth and expertise of the doctors and staff, showcasing the practice's inviting feel before patients even walked through the door.
  • SEO & Local Search Optimization: From targeted keyword strategies to a fully optimized Google My Business (GMB) Profile, we positioned Shore Smiles at the top of search results.
  • High-Impact Google Ads: To further fuel growth, we deployed strategic paid advertising to capture high-value patients at an exceptionally low cost per lead.
