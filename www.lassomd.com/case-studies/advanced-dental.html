The Challenge

Reaching New, High-Value Patients

Despite having a strong digital presence, including a fully functional website with engaging patient and staff video content, clear calls-to-action, and a well-executed local SEO strategy targeting both the Berlin and Cromwell markets, Advanced Clinic was still missing out on key high-value patient groups.

In particular, they wanted to attract more patients seeking emergency and same-day clinic care. Capturing these high-intent cases before the competition was critical, not only for continued growth but also to support the addition of new associates.

To bridge this gap and drive consistent, high-quality new patient flow, they turned to ClinicSoftware.ai to refine and optimize their new patient acquisition strategy.

The Solution

A Data-Driven Strategy for Maximum Return

To bring in more of the right new patients, ClinicSoftware.ai launched a highly targeted Google Ads campaign focusing on key patients needing emergency and same-day clinic services. In particular, those actively searching for immediate relief, such as those with chipped and broken teeth or those requiring extractions.

Detailed geographic targeting ensured that ads reached not only their core Berlin & Cromwell neighborhoods but also surrounding markets like Middletown, Wallingford, Meriden and Hartford. Negative targeting was also critical to filter out searches related to state-sponsored insurance and refine the focus to an audience most likely to have preferred PPO coverage.

To maximize conversions, ClinicSoftware.ai built a new, optimized landing page highlighting the warmth and comfort of the office, placing critical patient testimonial videos above the fold, and emphasizing the key actions patients needed to take—namely, calling the office and booking an appointment.

To enhance the effectiveness of the campaign beyond the initial ad click, the team integrated ClinicSoftware.ai’s AI-powered ClinicSoftware lead management software. This allowed for seamless tracking of patient inquiries, ensuring that every potential lead was followed up with efficiently, and that front desk interactions were optimized for maximum conversion.

The Results

5X ROAS and +14 New Patient Bookings

The campaign delivered immediate and measurable results. Within just a 30 day span, the team booked more than 14 new patient appointments at an incredibly low $140 Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC). The ads achieved an impressive 4%+ Click-Through Rate (CTR) at a highly competitive $10 Cost-Per-Click (CPC), significantly outperforming industry benchmarks. These new patients generated double-digit new patient revenue in their first appointments alone, producing a Return-on-Ad-Spend (ROAS) of more than 5X.

The success didn’t stop there. Dr. Maroon and the ClinicSoftware.ai team continue to meet regularly to evaluate and refine the performance of their paid advertising campaigns. By leveraging AI-powered insights and digital recordings of front desk interactions through ClinicSoftware.ai’s ClinicSoftware lead management software, they track each click from origination all the way to the operatory chair. Using AI-driven call coaching to train their front office staff, they have been able to continually optimize the efficacy of their new patient acquisition strategy, making the most of every inquiry.
