Do I own my website?

Yes, you own your website and all of its content, ensuring your SEO efforts continue to generate long-term value and new patient acquisition even if you change providers. This protects your rankings, authority, and digital equity over time.

Can I edit my own website content?

Yes, you can edit your website content at any time, and our team is also available to assist with updates. Most content change requests are completed within 48 hours, ensuring your website stays current and responsive to your practice’s needs. Regularly updating service pages, FAQs, and location content also helps maintain strong SEO performance and improves your visibility in both traditional search results and AI-driven search platforms.

Is my website custom designed or based on a template?

Every ClinicSoftware.ai website is fully custom designed—we do not use templates. As a clinic-specific marketing company, we build the site architecture, content structure, and technical SEO foundation specifically around your practice’s services and growth goals, helping your website rank higher in both traditional and AI-driven search results and convert more high-intent visitors into new patients.

Can you integrate patient forms, scheduling, or payment options?

Yes, we are happy to assist you in embedding relevant tools and modules into your site to achieve your desired functionality.

Depending on your readiness, kickoff to launch typically takes between 4-8 weeks.
