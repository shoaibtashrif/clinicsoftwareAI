ClinicSoftware.ai website and AI reception questions

What does ClinicSoftware.ai manage?

ClinicSoftware.ai manages the patient-facing website, approved virtual receptionist knowledge and scheduling connections as one service.

Can the website connect to our current scheduler?

Yes. The preferred approach is to connect the scheduling experience the clinic already uses rather than replace it.

How does the AI receptionist answer questions?

It uses clinic-approved information such as hours, services, first-visit expectations, policies and scheduling instructions to produce natural answers.

Can we launch before filming clinic-specific video?

Yes. A preview can use appropriate stock media and later replace it with simple clinic photography or short, professionally edited phone footage.

Does ClinicSoftware store clinical records?

No. The platform is a public marketing, information and scheduling layer. Clinical records remain in the clinic’s established systems.

Which types of clinics can use the platform?

The core system supports clinic, optometry, chiropractic, physical therapy and a broad range of independent specialty practices.

Can each clinic have a different visual direction?

Yes. Shared production components make delivery efficient while typography, color, imagery, video, copy and page composition adapt to the practice.

How quickly can a practice see a mock-up?

The system is designed to produce a focused prospect direction within days after receiving the current website and basic practice information.

What happens after the website launches?

ClinicSoftware can manage routine content changes, new landing pages and virtual receptionist knowledge updates as the practice evolves.
